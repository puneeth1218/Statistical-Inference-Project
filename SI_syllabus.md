### Syllabus:



Parametric point estimation:

(1)  Sampling distributions (revision).  \[2 Lectures]

(2)  Properties of estimator : Unbiasedness, consistency, efficiency, invariance.\[3 Lectures]

(3)  Methods of Estimation: Method of moments and maximum likelihood.\[3 Lectures]

(4)  Lower bounds of variance:  Frechet-Rao-Cramer, Bhattacharya, Chapman-Robbins-Kiefer lower bounds.  \[3 Lectures]

(5) Data reduction : Sufficiency, Factorization Theorem, Fisher’s Information, minimal sufficiency, ancillary statistic, completeness and bounded completeness, Basu’s Theorem. \[5 Lectures]

(6)  Improving estimators: Rao-Blackwell Theorem, Lehmann-Scheffe Theorem,UMVUE \[4 Lectures]



Test of hypothesis:

(7) Hypothesis: Simple and composite hypotheses, types of error. \[2 Lectures]

(8)  Construction \& comparison of tests: Neyman-Pearson Lemma, UMP, UMP unbiased and UMP invariant tests. \[6 Lectures]

(9)  Likelihood ratio tests: applications to one sample and two sample problems, Chi-square tests. Sequential probability ratio test, Families with monotone likelihood ratio. \[6 Lectures]



Confidence interval estimation:

(10)  Interval estimation: Methods for finding confidence intervals, shortest length confidence intervals. Bootstrap method for confidence interval.  \[6 Lectures]

A clear description of the data with its source

The real-world problem or challenge being addressed

The methodology, including appropriate estimation and testing procedures
